# June 2026 OPTC Team Builder Release Notes

Published: 2026-07-01

June focused on making OPTC Team Builder easier to trust during real team-building work: clearer explanations, stronger comparison flows, safer saved-team handling, and better captain coverage.

The release also tightened the maintainer path behind the app with repeatable QA, performance evidence, release-trigger checks, and searchable audit links for future work.

## What Shipped

### Highlights

The most visible improvements were concentrated around team-building confidence and data correctness.

- **Why-picked explanations are easier to verify** - Auto Team Builder recommendations now have stronger explanation coverage so users can understand why a character made the team. Evidence: [869dbnqv2](https://app.clickup.com/t/90121749478/869dbnqv2).
- **Captain coverage handles tiered requirements** - Tier-based captain coverage was validated end to end so captain ability filters and team summaries stay aligned with harder requirements. Evidence: [869dc7jtw](https://app.clickup.com/t/90121749478/869dc7jtw).
- **Captain boost parsing is more accurate** - Multi-stat and condition-gated captain boosts were corrected so generated metadata and runtime filters agree. Evidence: [869dwc0wd](https://app.clickup.com/t/90121749478/869dwc0wd).
- **Team comparison has durable QA coverage** - Compare-mode behavior was checked with release evidence so users can compare candidate teams with less regression risk. Evidence: [869dbnquz](https://app.clickup.com/t/90121749478/869dbnquz).
- **Saved-team import, export, and share flows were rechecked** - Saved-team transfer workflows were validated so teams can move between devices and sessions more reliably. Evidence: [869dbnqv3](https://app.clickup.com/t/90121749478/869dbnqv3).

- **Explanation and compare rendering received performance guardrails** - Heavy explanation and compare views were measured and optimized to keep interactions responsive. Evidence: [869dvr7x5](https://app.clickup.com/t/90121749478/869dvr7x5).
- **Ability-filter performance was measured** - Saved Teams, Saved Enemies, and Manual Team Builder filters now have repeatable timing evidence. Evidence: [869dvr7x6](https://app.clickup.com/t/90121749478/869dvr7x6).
- **Performance budgets became a recurring signal** - The browser performance harnesses now feed a repeatable budget report instead of one-off local checks. Evidence: [869dwc0hh](https://app.clickup.com/t/90121749478/869dwc0hh).
